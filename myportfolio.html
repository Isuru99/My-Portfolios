About

work at the intersection of cybersecurity and machine learning — two disciplines that, combined, produce more capable and resilient defensive systems than either achieves alone. My MSc dissertation extends a 2018 ANN baseline to reduce false positives in deep learning-based malware detection using memory forensics, applied to the CIC-MalMem-2022 dataset in PyTorch.

Before Plymouth, I spent time as an IT Administrator at Fcode Labs, supporting ISO 27001 compliance activities, managing endpoint security, and building an asset management tool with Microsoft Power Apps. I also completed hands-on penetration testing at Hacktify Cyber Security — web application vulnerabilities, red teaming, and CTF challenges — alongside IT engineering roles at Kingslake and South Asian Technologies.

I care about work that is honest about its limitations, measurable in its impact, and documented clearly enough to be understood by someone else.
